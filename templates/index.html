How It Works

A streamlined 3-step workflow for institutional attendance management.

1
Register & Map Face

Add student details, phone numbers, and capture their facial biometric profiles directly through the secure registration portal.

2
Scan & Mark Attendance

Open the live recognition scanner. The system instantly detects faces, matches embeddings against RAM cache, and logs attendance securely.

3
Automate & Report

Unrecognized or absent records trigger automated SMS notifications at 10:00 AM, while administrators review comprehensive analytics.
